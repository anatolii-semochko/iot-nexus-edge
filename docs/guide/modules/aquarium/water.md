---
title: "Water and CO2"
description: "CO2, dosing, water changes, filters and probes."
order: 2
---

- **CO2** - by pH (the drop from the morning baseline) or the whole window;
  never in the dark; the cylinder's pressure and what is left (counted from
  the bubbles for a liquid cylinder).
- **Dosing** - one dose per channel a day at the schedule's time; bottle
  levels, refill warnings.
- **Water change** - the day's fresh water in equal doses; the fresh-water
  filter's cartridges.
- **Filters** - internal ones with their pumps; an external loop with a
  permanent bio-filter and optional filters switched in and out together.
- **Probes** - pH, EC, NO3, NH4, O2, temperatures; step-by-step calibration;
  manual tests.
