![The weather scene](screen:weather)

The weather module joins the station's readings (temperature, wind, light,
rain, humidity, pressure) with the forecast fetched by the server
(Open-Meteo). The scene shows the warnings ahead (frost, storm gusts, heavy
rain, heat), now and the sun's day, the next hours and days - the sun for the
PV (kWh/m²) and the heating's degree-days. The energy plan and the heating
planner take their forecasts from it.

Settings - the location, the refresh, the scene's thresholds (frost, storm
gusts, heavy rain, heat, a good / fair sun day, the heating base).

![The weather's settings](screen:config-weather)

The settings - Processes -> Weather Node -> the settings button: the place and its coordinates, the forecast's days and refresh, the light's zones, the devices.
