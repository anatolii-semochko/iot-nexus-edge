## What it learns

- The PV forecast corrected by what the panels really gave this month (the
  shading, the soiling, the forecast's bias); the wind forecast by what the
  turbines gave.
- The consumption forecast corrected by weekday and hour.
- The similar days for the strategy's choice.

## The review and the savings

- **The day's review** - the day as it ran against the best strategy on the
  day as it really was (a perfect forecast): what could have been saved, split
  into the forecast's part and the strategy's part.
- **The savings bridge** - what you would have paid without the system, and
  what you paid.
- **The calendar** - a day, a month, a year; the key figures: saved, paid,
  self-sufficiency, own use of generation, peak import, battery cycles, the
  forecast's accuracy.
- **Accounts** - the private, business and tenants' consumption (electricity
  and heat), with what each tenant consumed and is to pay.
