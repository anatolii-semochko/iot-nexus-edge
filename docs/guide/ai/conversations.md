---
title: "Conversations"
description: "Starting a conversation, the AI button, sharing."
order: 1
---

- The **AI Assistant** page: pick what to talk about (the whole system or an
  instance) and the work type from the tiles - analyse, configure, install,
  watch, report, ...; the topic may change on the way.
- The **AI button** on a card or a scene opens the conversation about that
  object in the side panel.
- Someone else's conversation is read only; a conversation can be shared
  (read only for the others who may talk to that assistant).
