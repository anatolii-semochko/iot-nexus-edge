![The Nodes page](screen:nodes)

Every node of the installation with its type, status and last heartbeat. A
search, a status filter (all, error, simulation) and groups narrow the list.

- **Status** - online, overdue (no heartbeat within its limit), error; a
  simulated node is marked so.
- **Physical / simulated** - switch a node between its real hardware and its
  simulated twin, live (when the twin is provisioned).
- **The row** - expand it for the node's devices; the settings (name, group,
  parent node, the maker's model, its photo, the place on the map); the Library
  link of its type.
- **Groups** - node groups for the business view (a building, a tank).

## A node's settings

- **Name**, **Group** (the node groups), **Attached to** - the node it plugs
  into (a building's controller, a bus master) or **Not attached**;
- **Model** - the maker's model of the real unit (its Library type stays);
- **Photo of the unit** - the real one, shown in its popup's Photo tab;
  **Remove the current photo**;
- **Position on the map** - latitude and longitude (both or none), shown in
  the popup's Map tab.
