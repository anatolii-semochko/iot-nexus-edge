![The notification centre](screen:notifications)

The header's three icons count the new messages, the active warnings and
errors; a click opens the notification centre - the new ones first, all,
unread; mark as read; open the object. The levels decide the sound and the
LEDs on an operator's node; the groups - who receives them.
