The Dashboard is a tree of cards:

- a **folder** - its cards on a level of their own (e.g. Energy, Heating,
  Aquariums);
- an **avatar** - a small live picture of a component; it opens the
  component on the whole page;
- a **component** - drawn at the level's full width under its blocks (e.g.
  the energy overview).

What a person sees follows their role: a card shows only where the role sees
its object; a folder - only with something inside.

## Arranging the cards

Service -> Config -> Dashboard (for those allowed to configure it) lists a
level as the Dashboard shows it: the cards (folders and avatars - with their
picture and background) and, under them, the components - each list ordered
by itself. **Config** - the Dashboard's own settings: the scale of the cards on
the top level. Add a folder, an avatar or a component; move them up and down;
edit a card:

- the **picture** - none, the component's avatar, an icon from the set, or an
  own image (any size - a large image is shrunk on upload), with its size;
- a **background** with its size and opacity;
- the **frame** - borders at the top, the middle line, the bottom, the fill;
- for a folder - the **cards scale** of the cards inside it (0.50 - 2.00; 1.00 -
  as usual, 1.50 - half as large again: fewer, larger cards in a row);
- for an avatar - which avatar, and whether the viewer may switch between
  fitting the width and the height.

The preview in the form is as wide as the card on the Dashboard in your
window, so a picture is cropped the same.
