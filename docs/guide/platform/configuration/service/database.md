---
title: "Database"
description: "System stamps - save, download, apply the configuration; the logs - download and clear."
order: 2
---

![Service - Database](screen:service-database)

- **System stamps** - save the configuration (devices, nodes, processes,
  users, groups) as a state; download it as a file; apply a saved one or upload
  one back - with a compatibility check first. Applying replaces the whole
  configuration; the logs stay.
- **Logs** - download them, or clear them up to a date (per object, with the
  volumes shown first). Active warnings and pending proposals are kept.
