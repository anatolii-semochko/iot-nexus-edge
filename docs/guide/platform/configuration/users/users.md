![Users](screen:users)

Users -> Users: add a person (username, name, email, password), give them
roles, set their AI tokens a day (empty - unlimited, 0 - the AI off),
deactivate. Service accounts (the orchestrator, the simulator, the AI agent)
and the guest are built in.

An MCP client's account (`mcp-...`, made with its token - AI Assistant ->
Connections and MCP) is listed here too: no password, it never signs in; its
roles are set here like a person's (never a role with every right).
