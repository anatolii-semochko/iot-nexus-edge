![The Logs page](screen:logs)

The tabs:

- **Commands** - every command with its author (a person, a process, the AI,
  the orchestrator), its target and result;
- **Devices** - the logged device values;
- **Messages** - the processes' warnings, errors and messages;
- **AI Proposals** - what the assistant proposed, accepted or rejected, by whom;
- **AI Reports** - the reports to the developers.

A filter by date, object and text on each; download the logs or clear them in
Service -> Database.
