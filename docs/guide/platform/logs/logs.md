![The Logs page](screen:logs)

The tabs:

- **Commands** - every command with its author (a person, a process, the AI,
  the orchestrator), its target and result;
- **Devices** - the logged device values;
- **Messages** - the processes' warnings, errors and messages;
- **AI Proposals** - what the assistant proposed, accepted or rejected, by whom;
- **AI Reports** - the reports to the developers;
- **AI usage** - the AI's tokens by day: the guests, the scheduled runs and
  the people stacked, the day limits as dashed lines (the guests' shared
  budget, each connection's); a bar names its figures on hover, the table
  under it has the exact numbers; 7, 30 or 90 days;
- **MCP calls** - every tool an MCP client called: the time, the token and its
  account, the tool, how long, an error; a row opens the call's input.

![The AI usage tab](screen:logs-ai-usage)

A filter by date, object and text on each; download the logs or clear them in
Service -> Database.
