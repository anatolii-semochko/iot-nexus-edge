Nexus Edge runs real equipment on site - energy, heating, aquariums, any
automated system - and learns from its own data, with an AI assistant at
every step. It is one platform with layers that share the same data: the
devices and their buses, the model of the installation, the processes that
control, the simulator, the web interface and the AI.
