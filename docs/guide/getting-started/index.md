## Signing in

Open the system's address in a browser and sign in with your username and
password. If the guest is switched on, the system opens without signing in,
in the guest's role; **Sign in** in the user menu takes you to the sign-in
page.

## The interface

![The Dashboard with the menu and the header](screen:dashboard)

- **The menu** on the left - Dashboard, AI Assistant, Devices (Nodes,
  Devices, Simulator), Orchestration (Processes), Logs (Live Events, Logs),
  Settings (Service, Users, Library, Docs). It shows what your role allows; a
  role may show the Dashboard only - then there is no menu.
- **The header** - the Simulation badge (some node is simulated), the system
  tick, the messages, warnings and errors (the notification centre), the
  language, the theme, your role now and the user menu.
- **The pages** - lists with a search and filters, expandable rows, tabs; a
  list remembers its page, filters and sorting in your browser.
